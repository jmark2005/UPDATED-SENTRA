About the System

What is SENTRA?

SENTRA is a project by Global Solutions INC. designed to centralize and streamline all activities related to student safety incidents and student support services within a designated school.

The system tracks safety incidents, semester attendance, and support services while maintaining clear records of participation by administrators, teachers, counselors, and other school personnel.

By providing a digital dashboard and facility mapping, SENTRA enables faster response times and informed decision-making at every level of school management.

Administrators

Full dashboard access to monitor, manage, and respond to all incidents and user accounts.

Teachers & Staff

Submit reports, view assigned tasks, and coordinate support activities efficiently.

Students

Access support resources, trigger emergency alerts, and monitor their own attendance.

Counselors

Track student welfare activities and document support service completion records.

Core Functionalities

What the System Does

Student Information Management

Records and manages essential student information needed for safety monitoring, attendance tracking, and student support services.

Safety Incident Reporting & Monitoring

Allows designated school personnel to record, update, and monitor student safety incidents — including incident details, date, people involved, actions taken, and current status.

Incident Action & Resolution Tracking

Tracks the progress and completion of actions taken in response to reported safety incidents, and identifies incidents that still require follow-up.

Semester Attendance Monitoring

Records and monitors student attendance throughout the semester — including present, absent, and tardy statuses — and generates attendance summaries.

Student Support Services Management

Records student support activities such as academic assistance, guidance referrals, and other school-based support services within the project's scope.

Support Activity Progress Tracking

Monitors the status of student support activities — including pending, ongoing, and completed services — to ensure appropriate follow-up for every student.

Emergency Alert

Enables students and staff to instantly trigger emergency notifications to school management for fast on-campus response.

Facility Mapping

An interactive virtual diagram of the school premises that pinpoints the exact location of reported incidents or ongoing emergencies.

Definition of Terms

Key Concepts & Terminology

Administrator (Admin)

An authorized user — a school official or appointed staff — with unrestricted access to the system's dashboard to monitor, manage, and respond to reported incidents and user accounts.

Dashboard

The main portal used by administrators and school staff to monitor, manage, and address reported incidents and student concerns.

Emergency Alert

A system feature that enables students or staff to notify school management of emergencies occurring on school premises.

Facility Mapping

A virtual diagram of the school premises integrated into the system to indicate the location of reported incidents or emergencies.

Incident Reporting

The process of submitting reports on safety issues or events occurring on school premises through the system.

Login / Authentication

The process by which users (students, teachers, staff, administrators) verify their identity — typically via username and password — to gain authorized access to the system.
